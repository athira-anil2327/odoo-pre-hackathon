# Subscription Management System

**Group Name:** Falcons

## Problem Statement

Modern subscription-based businesses struggle to manage recurring billing, customer accounts, and revenue tracking when relying on disconnected tools, spreadsheets, and manual workflows. Without an integrated solution, operations face several critical challenges:

* **Manual Tracking & Human Error:** Customer plans, renewals, and billing cycles are tracked in spreadsheets, leading to missed renewals, delayed billing, and lost recurring revenue.
* **Disconnected Billing & Invoicing:** Invoices are manually typed every month, tax calculations are prone to inconsistency, and payments must be painstakingly matched against bank statements.
* **Access & Role Separation:** Customers lack self-service access to view or manage their subscriptions and invoices, while internal staff and administrators lack clear role boundaries and centralized management.
* **Reporting Bottlenecks:** Determining key metrics—such as total recurring revenue, active subscription counts, and overdue balances—requires compiling disjointed spreadsheets, delaying vital financial decisions.

### Objective

To build a unified, automated subscription management platform that oversees the complete lifecycle of a subscription business. The system handles product and plan setup, quotation-to-subscription workflows, automated recurring invoicing, payment reconciliation, discount and tax management, and real-time revenue analytics.
